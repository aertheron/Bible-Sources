# Word and theme studies

An expanding collection for Mark Gatzen's Source Text Bible Study Teacher. The first 50 studies are the enriched 0.2.0 collection prepared on 5 October 2026 and published here on 6 October. The collection can grow beyond 50.

Each study combines local reading, a contextual translation key, recurring misunderstandings, biblical development, canonical connections and a theology check. Scripture and the actual clause generate the argument. The project's working biblical and canonical theology supplies a preferred, revisable synthesis. Lexical range, contextual meaning and theological inference remain distinct; established theological systems do not supply the default conclusions.

## Use a study

Choose the relevant entry below, or use the [lookup index](../../indexes/word-study-index.json). The index maps study IDs, titles, English/Dutch aliases and base Strong numbers to individual studies and exact structured-record locations. A base identifier returns candidates; it does not choose a contextual sense or resolve homonyms.

Retrieve one relevant study, or at most two for a normal comparison. Select no more than six relevant context pointers within a 12,000-character packet. Related entries and the complete passage bank are not loaded automatically. Check the source form, syntax, genre and local passage before applying a default explanation. The future runtime must enforce these limits; this repository provides data rather than an installed tool.

| `sources/source_register.json` | Attributed preparation sources and their actual access/review status |
| [Existing lexical sources](../../sources/lexical/) | The 606 supporting records; reused rather than duplicated |
| [UPDATING.md](UPDATING.md) | How to extend or revise the collection |
| [ENTRY_TEMPLATE.md](ENTRY_TEMPLATE.md) | A starting structure for a completed study |
| [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md) | Source terms, authored material and public-edition changes |

Preparation hashes remain distinct from published book-file hashes. Internal file identifiers are replaced by verified public book paths. The historical unresolved Greek OT preparation pointer contains no corpus or excerpt here; shared use must consult an identified permitted Greek edition. BibleProject and other linked resources are research leads with stated access history, not imported commentary collections.

## Review state

The collection is original model-assisted study material prepared for Mark. Selected lexical records and consequential source forms were checked during preparation. Contextual and theological synthesis remains explicitly identified; independent scholarly peer review and complete Greek sense tagging are not claimed. Keep the strongest relevant challenge visible and revise a conclusion when its textual support fails.
