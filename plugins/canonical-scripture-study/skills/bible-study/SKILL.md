# Canonical Scripture Study

Resolve the user's command with study_plan or the CLI plan. For a bare passage, COMPLETE the Standard study (working translation, relevant notes and concise explanation); a plan or orientation alone is not the requested result. Honour Standard, Detailed or Full from the returned study_depth and response_sections; retain the focused command rather than turning it into a full study. For an explicit command, complete that task without forcing all the prose of a full study. Normalize ordinary phrasing into the documented command when necessary.

## Visible delivery and autonomous completion

Treat research stages and user-visible delivery as distinct. Retrieve and verify source evidence before presenting a claim as settled. Follow the returned delivery.milestones in order; a milestone is ready only when its ready_when condition holds. For a normal passage: (1) offer a one- or two-sentence orientation without dumping the internal plan; (2) publish the full working translation and necessary textual/translation notes once the wording is checked; (3) publish local exegesis, meaningful names/terms and literary structure; (4) publish bounded canonical and theological synthesis. In Standard, keep each part concise. For a focused word, text-variant, DSS or context task, follow its own returned milestones; never force a translation it did not request.

Prefer separate visible assistant messages for completed milestones if the host explicitly supports multiple messages within one turn; do not hold the translation until the entire study is finished. After displaying a milestone, autonomously continue the remaining applicable research within the same user request. The reader does not have to type Continue, Next or another question to finish the current bounded passage. Do not ask for confirmation between milestones unless genuinely necessary. Next always advances to another passage, never to the next delivery milestone.

If the host does not support successive assistant messages, give one progressively streamed answer, in the same evidence-first order with clear section breaks. Do not claim that the plugin can send multiple chat messages on its own, invent progress or imply tool calls/background jobs will continue after the assistant response ends. Do not publish a preliminary translation as final before reviewing consequential variants; preserve uncertainties and correct an earlier interim statement explicitly if subsequent evidence changes it.

Follow scope_policy before research: ordinary passage studies use the smallest supported enclosing literary unit. Explain the adjustment and its purpose immediately—for Genesis 1, include 1:1–2:3 because the seventh day completes the account. Complete that selected unit, using smaller sequential retrieval windows when needed. An explicit request for only particular verses uses the comma option “exact”; keep its focus while explaining the surrounding unit. The curated index is not exhaustive: always review the actual discourse boundary and replan a supported unindexed unit with “exact” if needed. Focused translation, variant, DSS and other commands retain their requested task.

Default to English unless the user explicitly requests another language or a retained continuation_state specifies it. Do not infer Dutch merely from the user’s location, profile or unrelated previous chats. Read the translation policy before settling wording; preserve source-supported OT–NT verbal connections and explain consequential traditional/scholarly alternatives in footnotes.

Retain the returned continuation_state. Process only the current portion; show the logical Next reference briefly. For larger requests, complete the first portion now and preserve all pending references. On Next, pass the state back so the mode, study depth, language and format persist. Do not refuse a valid multi-chapter or whole-book study merely for length, and do not restart the whole request. For oversized dense packets, subdivide into contiguous portions and preserve the remainder.

Treat literary boundaries as meaningful for both context and continuation. Before suggesting Next, check the passage's enclosing curated literary unit, its internal rhetorical structure, and the selected depth. Where the bounded remainder fits the source and verse limits, recommend the remaining verses through the end of that unit—even if the unit crosses a chapter boundary (e.g., after Genesis 1:1–3 suggest Genesis 1:4–2:3, not Genesis 1:4–31). When the remainder is too large, prefer a defensible smaller discourse division; retain bounded chapter/chunk fallback when no supported unit is available. Label the reason for a curated suggestion; do not pretend the unit index is exhaustive or that every chapter boundary is literary. Explicit exact ranges and explicit Next chapter/Next: references take precedence. Explain automatic study-scope adjustments through scope_policy; an explicit exact request and focused commands keep their scope.

Explain a dependent opening from retrieved prior discourse before translation. Use the nearby preceding passage as a starting window, not a proof that the previous five verses capture the whole argument. Identify the source of therefore/so/for and comparable connectors at the relevant literary/discourse scale, sometimes the preceding paragraph or larger argument. Review provisional size boundaries against literary flow.

Focused word/theme commands use their own logical structure, not a full passage-translation template. Word studies default to concise Standard; accept Full word study / volledige woordstudie or a depth option for developed research. Full word studies follow the returned semantic_method checklist: cover all supported attested senses (including rare or separate homographs), explain the sense at each occurrence with participants and relationships, compare neighboring concepts, and only then make distinct canonical and theological conclusions. The 4–6 diagnostic verse target is not a limit on sense coverage. Apply word_study_policy and its citation_policy; verifiable lexicon attributions require the actual entry, not an assumption from our curated study.  Retrieve one or two complete word studies and at most six relevant context/anchor units; do not recurse through the whole canon.
